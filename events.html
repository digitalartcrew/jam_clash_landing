How It Works

The Jam Clash Live Format

Doors Open — T minus 90 min

Early Admission Guests Arrive

Jam Clash Pro members get early access to the venue. Venue doors open 90 minutes before the main event. Bar is open, energy is building.

T minus 75 min

Teams Form & the Clash Begins

Guests pair up into teams on their iPhones — no equipment, no setup. The Jam Clash host kicks off the first round of music trivia battles. Real iTunes song previews play. Rounds are fast and competitive.

30–45 min of gameplay

The Music Battle

Teams compete across multiple genres — hip hop, R&B, pop, classics, and more. The crowd watches, reacts, and gets loud. The bar is buzzing. Everyone's having a moment before the night officially starts.

Champions Crowned

Winners Announced

The winning team gets recognition — bragging rights, a shoutout, or venue-sponsored prizes. By now, guests are warmed up, connected, and ready for the main event. The venue is packed and the energy is locked in.

Main Event Begins

Seamless Handoff

Jam Clash wraps 30+ minutes before the headliner or main event starts. The room is already full, the crowd is already vibing, and the venue keeps the momentum going all night.
