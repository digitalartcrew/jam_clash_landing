The Format

How the Night Runs

Doors Open — 90 min early

Pro Members Get In First

Flash your Jam Clash Pro membership at the door. Early access, bar is open, the room is yours before the crowd arrives.

15 min after doors

Teams Form

Pair up with friends — or strangers. Open your Jam Clash app, join the event lobby, and pick your team name.

30–45 min of gameplay

The Clash

Rounds across multiple genres. Hip hop, R&B, pop, throwbacks, and more. Guess the song before the other team does. First to the target score wins.

Champions Crowned

The top team gets their moment. Then the main event kicks off and the whole room is already warmed up and locked in.
